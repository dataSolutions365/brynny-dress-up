# Where the Christmas town songs come from

These play one after another while you walk around town at Christmas (`XMAS_TOWN_SONGS` in `3d/seasons.js`).
Every one is a recording made by a United States military band (Air Force or Army). Works made by the US government are in the
public domain, so they are free to use, change and share. Each file was shortened for phones
(mono, 48 kbps, at most 2½ minutes, volume evened out).

Only add songs here that are free to use. Newer songs like "Grandma Got Run Over by a Reindeer" or
"Driving Home for Christmas" are still under copyright and can't go in the game.
